## Requirement

Servers are created with `http.Server` and set at least `ReadHeaderTimeout`, plus `ReadTimeout`,
`WriteTimeout` and `IdleTimeout` appropriate to the endpoint. `http.ListenAndServe` without a
configured server is not used in services.
