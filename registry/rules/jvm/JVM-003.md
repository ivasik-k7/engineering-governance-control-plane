## Requirement

`management.endpoints.web.exposure.include` lists endpoints explicitly (normally `health,info`)
and never `*`. `heapdump`, `env`, `configprops`, `threaddump` and `loggers` are not exposed over
HTTP, and `management.endpoint.env.show-values` is not `ALWAYS`.
