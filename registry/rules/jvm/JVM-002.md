## Requirement

Every `DocumentBuilderFactory`, `SAXParserFactory`, `XMLInputFactory`, `TransformerFactory` and
`SchemaFactory` disables DOCTYPE declarations
(`http://apache.org/xml/features/disallow-doctype-decl`) or, where DTDs are required, external
entities and external DTD access (`ACCESS_EXTERNAL_DTD`, `ACCESS_EXTERNAL_SCHEMA`).
