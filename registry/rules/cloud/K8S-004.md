## Requirement

Every container, including init containers, has an effective `runAsNonRoot: true` (or a non-zero
`runAsUser`) and `readOnlyRootFilesystem: true`. Writable paths are `emptyDir` volumes.
`runAsNonRoot` is required by the restricted Pod Security Standard; a read-only root filesystem
is a CIS Kubernetes Benchmark and NSA/CISA hardening recommendation.

## Validation notes

The container `securityContext` overrides the pod `securityContext` for `runAsNonRoot` and
`runAsUser`; `readOnlyRootFilesystem` exists only at container level. `runAsUser: 0` is reported
even with `runAsNonRoot: true`, since the kubelet then refuses to start the container.
