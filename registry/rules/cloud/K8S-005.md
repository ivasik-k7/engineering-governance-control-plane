## Requirement

`kind: Secret` manifests in the repository carry no `data` or `stringData` values in plain form;
base64 is an encoding, not encryption. Secrets come from an External Secrets Operator
`ExternalSecret`, a Sealed Secrets `SealedSecret`, or a SOPS-encrypted manifest.

## Validation notes

Not reported: documents with a top-level `sops:` key, values in SOPS `ENC[…]` form, Helm template
expressions (`{{ … }}`) and whole-value `${VAR}` substitutions resolved at deploy time, and empty
values. `SealedSecret` and `ExternalSecret` are different kinds and are never reported.
