## Requirement

No ingress rule open to `0.0.0.0/0` or `::/0` allows all protocols (`-1`), the full port range
0–65535, or a range covering an administrative, database, cache or broker port: 22 (SSH),
3389 (RDP), 3306 (MySQL), 5432 (PostgreSQL), 1433 (SQL Server), 1521 (Oracle), 27017 (MongoDB),
6379 (Redis), 9200 (Elasticsearch/OpenSearch), 5601 (Kibana), 2375/2376 (Docker daemon),
11211 (Memcached), 5984 (CouchDB) and 9092 (Kafka).

This covers inline `ingress` blocks of `aws_security_group`, `aws_security_group_rule` with
`type = "ingress"` and `aws_vpc_security_group_ingress_rule`. Public web ports such as 80 and 443
are allowed. Administrative access goes through SSM Session Manager, a VPN or a bastion with a
narrow allow-list.
