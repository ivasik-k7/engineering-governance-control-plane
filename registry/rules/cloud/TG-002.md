## Requirement

A `remote_state` with `backend = "s3"` sets `encrypt = true` in its `config` and locks state with
`use_lockfile = true` (S3 conditional writes, Terraform and OpenTofu 1.10 or later) or a
`dynamodb_table`. State holds secrets in clear text, and concurrent unlocked runs corrupt it.
