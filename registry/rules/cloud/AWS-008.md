## Requirement

Terraform does not create `aws_iam_access_key` or `aws_iam_user_login_profile` resources. Their
secrets land in state, and they do not expire. Workloads use IAM roles (instance profiles, IRSA or
EKS Pod Identity, task roles), CI uses OIDC federation, and people sign in through IAM Identity
Center. A third-party integration that only accepts access keys gets a waiver with an owner and a
rotation schedule.
