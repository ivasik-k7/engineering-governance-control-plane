## Requirement

Every `aws_cloudtrail` sets `is_multi_region_trail = true`, `enable_log_file_validation = true`
and a customer-managed `kms_key_id`. Both flags default to `false`, and without a KMS key the logs
are encrypted only with SSE-S3, so access to the bucket alone is enough to read them.

The organization trail is owned by the platform team in a dedicated log-archive account; workload
accounts do not need their own trail for compliance.
