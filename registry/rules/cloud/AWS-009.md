## Requirement

In production:

- `aws_db_instance` and `aws_rds_cluster` set `deletion_protection = true` and
  `backup_retention_period` of at least 7 days. The provider defaults leave deletion protection off
  and retention at 0 (instance) or 1 (cluster) days. Read replicas inherit backups from their source.
- `aws_dynamodb_table` sets `point_in_time_recovery { enabled = true }` and
  `deletion_protection_enabled = true`.

Longer retention, cross-region or cross-account copies (AWS Backup) follow the service's RPO.
