## Requirement

Scripts do not run downloaded content directly (`curl … | bash`, `bash <(curl …)`,
`eval "$(curl …)"`). They download a pinned version to a file, verify it against a recorded
checksum or signature, then run it, or install through a package manager.

## Validation notes

Applies the DOCKER-004 `RUN` check to `.sh` and `.bash` files, joining lines continued with `\`,
`|`, `&&` or `||`. Whole-line comments are skipped.
