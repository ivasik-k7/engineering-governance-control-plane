## Requirement

HTTP clients must not disable certificate or hostname verification: `verify=False` in Python;
trust-all `X509TrustManager`, `NoopHostnameVerifier` or `TrustAllStrategy` on the JVM;
`InsecureSkipVerify: true` in Go. Private CAs are configured by adding the CA to the trust store.
