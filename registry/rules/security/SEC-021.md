## Requirement

External programs are started with an argument list and no shell: `subprocess.run([...])` without
`shell=True` and no `os.system`; `ProcessBuilder` with separate arguments instead of `sh -c` or a
concatenated `Runtime.exec`; `exec.Command(name, args...)` instead of `sh -c`; `execFile` instead
of `exec` with interpolated strings.
